Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

MyShout keeps your groups, people and shouts on your phone. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send a message, share a link, or choose to back up to your account.

What is stored on your phone

Names, and the mobile numbers and email addresses you add or pick from your contacts; the groups you create; and each shout (who bought what, for which group, and when). You can edit or delete this at any time, or remove it all by uninstalling the app.

Contacts

If you choose "From contacts", MyShout reads your phone's contacts so you can pick people. Only the people you pick are saved in MyShout. Your contact list is never uploaded.

Microphone and speech

When listening for names, MyShout uses your phone's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your phone, the audio may be processed by the speech service provider (for example Google). MyShout does not record or keep audio.

Messages and share links

When you choose to tell the group, MyShout opens your own Messages or Mail app with the text ready; you decide whether to send it. Share links contain the group name, first names and the shout history so friends can import it. Anyone with the link can see that information.

Optional account and cloud backup

An account is optional. If you sign in with Google, Facebook, Apple or email, we store your email address and your backup file with our hosting provider (Supabase) so you can restore it later. Signing in with Google, Facebook or Apple is handled by them under their own privacy policies. You can stop using cloud backup and ask for your account and backups to be deleted at any time.

Update checks

While MyShout is in the background it may occasionally ask GitHub (where the app is published) whether a new version is out. That's a normal web request; no personal data is sent.

Children

MyShout is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the download page, with the date it was updated.
